How we validate it

Same reel, same group of three. Two ways to decide.

A · Today’s flow

  • Careem group order
  • Shortlist by price and distance
  • Host collects choices and chases payments

B · SAVD

  • Saves and group fit pick the restaurant
  • Everyone adds a meal and confirms a share
  • Host places one order

What we measure

  • Time from sharing the reel to placing the order
  • Host interventions (chasing, fixing, covering)
  • People left without a suitable meal

The assumption to test

A saved reel shows curiosity, not a plan. Start with reels members share or mark Want to try. Then test whether ordinary bookmarks improve the pick.

Early pass: 4 of 5 groups reach a choice with nobody left out.

Launch smart

Start small, inside one office, with what people choose to share.

Phase 1 · Office Lunch

  • One area, a few office groups
  • Shared reels and Want to try only
  • Fees split evenly, simulated first

Phase 2 · Learn taste

  • Save is curious, “I’d order this” is interested, reorder is liked
  • Users can correct what SAVD thinks

What production needs

  • Reel metadata: platform links and previews, with user-pasted links as the base case
  • Restaurant and menu matching to Careem Food listings

 

  • Delivery coverage by branch and address
  • Payments: per-person authorization, one captured order, partial refunds if a share changes
